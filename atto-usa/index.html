              Frequently asked questions

              What is in the file?

              Where and when to notify your airline, a page for flight day, a notification letter with your details, a manufacturer letter and the battery approvals.

              What details do you keep?

              None. Your details stay in your browser and are written into your file on your device. MovingLife does not receive or store them.

              Is the scooter approved for flights?

              The battery is a dry lithium-ion battery, 249.6 Wh, it meets IATA requirements and passed the UN 38.3 test. The final decision is made by the airline.

              My airline is not on the list

              Choose "Other airline" and you will get a general notification letter with all the scooter details.
